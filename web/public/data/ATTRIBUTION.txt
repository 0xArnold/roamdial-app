Land point cloud: generated from Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons (public domain).
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
License: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Sampling: 0.75 degree latitude bands, longitude spacing adjusted by cosine of latitude.
Radio station locations remain from the existing Radio Browser dataset; approximate locations are not geographic precision claims.

City anchors: GeoNames cities15000 (downloaded 2026-10-21).
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip
GeoNames data is licensed CC BY 4.0: https://www.geonames.org/about.html
Country-level station markers use deterministic illustrative city anchors, not verified station addresses.
